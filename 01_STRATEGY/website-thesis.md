## 1. THE SENTENCE

If the entire website had to be reduced to one sentence:

# **ATHLIMA IS A ROOM. THE WEBSITE IS THE DOOR.**

Not a brochure. Not an event microsite. Not a wellness club page.

The physical ATHLIMA is a deliberately small, curated room — 350 people at The St. Regis Mumbai on
14–15 December 2026. The scarcity is the product. The website's job is not to describe that room. It is to
make a visitor understand, in under ten seconds, that **the room exists, that it matters, and that they are
either in it or outside it.**

## 3. THE DIGITAL THESIS

The brochure's own line, from page 03, is the most useful strategic sentence anyone has written about
ATHLIMA:

> ### THE ECOSYSTEM IS ACTIVE. BUT NOT CONNECTED.

Every good decision on this website follows from taking that literally.

**If the problem is disconnection, the website must be an act of connection.**

That has three consequences that separate this site from a normal event site:

### 3.1 The site is organised around PEOPLE and RELATIONSHIPS, not pages and programme
A conventional event site leads with What / When / Where / Buy Tickets. ATHLIMA leads with **who is in the
room and what happens when they meet.** The programme is a supporting fact, not the headline.

### 3.2 The six IPs are portals, not pages
Page 04 of the brochure says it explicitly: *"Multiple IPs. One connected ecosystem."* The site must make
them feel like six doors into one building — visually and structurally related, sharing a spatial system,
never six unrelated landing pages with different layouts.

### 3.3 The site must answer "why should I care?" six different ways
Page 05 names four audience groups — SPORT, INSTITUTIONS, BUSINESS, CAPITAL. A founder, an athlete, a
federation secretary, a developer, an investor and a brand all need a different first sentence. Same
ecosystem. Different doorway. This is the single biggest reason a conventional sitemap fails here.

## 4. THE CREATIVE IDEA

## **ONE ROOM. ONE ECOSYSTEM. ONE SHARED FUTURE FOR SPORT.**

*(the brochure's own line, page 03 — it was already the answer)*

The site is built as a **descent into a room**.

The visitor arrives outside — the city, night, scale, ambition. Mumbai. The A as architecture, not as logo.
Then the site moves inward: from the country, to the industry, to the building, to the floor, to the six
spaces, to the people, to the individual conversation, to the application.

**Scale → Diagnosis → Ecosystem → The Six → The Room → Your Doorway → Provenance → The Next Generation
→ Invitation.**

That inward movement is the through-line for the homepage scroll, for the navigation model, for the motion
language, and for the photography brief. It is why the site never feels like a list of sections: every
scroll takes you one step further inside.

The counter-motion is the ATHLIMA **A** — which points *up*. Architecture rising as you go deeper. That
tension — descending into the room while the mark ascends — is the visual signature of the whole site.

## 5. WHAT THE WEBSITE MUST ACHIEVE

In strict priority order. When two goals conflict, the higher number wins.

| # | Goal | Measured by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Convert a qualified organisation into a Founding Partner conversation | Partner enquiries from named, relevant organisations |
| 2 | Convert a qualified individual into a guest application | Applications, and their quality |
| 3 | Establish ATHLIMA as institutional, not promotional | Qualitative: does a federation secretary or a family office take it seriously? |
| 4 | Make the six IPs legible as one ecosystem | Scroll depth to the portals; navigation into more than one IP per session |
| 5 | Give ATHLIMA a reason to exist after 15 December 2026 | Journal traffic, returning visitors, ATHLIMA 20 nominations |

**Goal 3 is the one that is easiest to lose and hardest to recover.** A site that looks like it is selling
will be read as selling. ATHLIMA is invitation-led; the website must behave like an institution that is
opening a door, not a business that is filling a hall.

## 6. THE PRIMARY TENSION — AND HOW WE RESOLVE IT

ATHLIMA has two irreconcilable-looking needs:

**A.** It is exclusive, capped at 350, invitation-led. Scarcity is the product.
**B.** It needs a public website that ranks, gets shared, and builds an audience for a year-round platform.

A site that is purely exclusive is invisible. A site that is purely open destroys the scarcity.

### The resolution: **OPEN THINKING. CLOSED ROOM.**

- **Never gated:** the thinking. The Journal, the Symposium themes, the diagnosis of Indian sport, the
  research, ATHLIMA 20 athlete stories, the films. This is what earns attention, links and authority.
- **Always gated:** the room. Attendance is by application. Partnership is by conversation. There is no
  "Buy Ticket" button anywhere on this website, at any price, ever.

The public content builds the reputation. The reputation makes the closed room worth applying to. That is
the entire growth model, and it is why the Journal is a strategic asset and not a content-marketing
afterthought.

## 7. WHAT THE WEBSITE IS NOT

Carried forward from the brochure's own filter, and binding:

- ❌ Not a digital version of the brochure
- ❌ Not an event ticketing site. **No pricing table. No "Buy Now". No "Register Here".**
- ❌ Not a trade-show site (no "exhibitor list", no "book your stall", no floor-plan-as-hero)
- ❌ Not a startup landing page (no feature grid, no "Trusted by" logo wall of unconfirmed brands)
- ❌ Not luxury-hospitality pastiche (no serif-and-gold, no "an unparalleled experience")
- ❌ Not a sports-media site (ATHLIMA is not covering sport; it is convening the people who build it)
- ❌ Not a personal brand vehicle
- ❌ Not a Web3/crypto aesthetic, despite the black-and-neon palette. **This is the single most likely
  failure mode of the visual system and is treated as a hard prohibition throughout this repository.**

## 8. THE STANDARD

Every screen, component, sentence and animation must pass all five:

1. **Does it belong to ATHLIMA?** Does it strengthen the platform, or is it decoration?
2. **Does it give a real role?** To a partner, a guest, an athlete — not just visibility.
3. **Does it create access or value?** People, ideas, business, participation or impact.
4. **Can it be executed beautifully?** Attention to detail is part of the proposition, not a finishing pass.
5. **Can it become something more?** Does it have a life beyond 15 December 2026?

*(These are the brochure's own five questions, from the master brief. They govern the website exactly as
they govern the event.)*

## 9. THE ONE-LINE BRIEF FOR THE HOMEPAGE

> A visitor who has never heard of ATHLIMA should understand, within ten seconds and without scrolling,
> that something serious and consequential is being built around Indian sport, that it is happening in
> Mumbai this December, and that getting into it is not automatic.

If the first screen does not do that, nothing further down the page will save it.
