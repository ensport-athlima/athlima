# BRAND STRATEGY

---

## 1. THE BRAND IDEA

# **SPORT CONNECTS. ATHLIMA CONNECTS SPORT.**

ATHLIMA's brand idea is not about sport itself. It is about **the connective tissue around sport** —
the relationships, capital, infrastructure, policy and technology without which talent does not become
an industry.

The brand is therefore not heroic in the way sports brands usually are. It does not say *go faster, push
harder, be your best.* It says something quieter and more institutional:

> **India has the talent. What it needs is the room.**

## 2. THE BRAND ARCHITECTURE

```
                        THE ENARR GROUP
                    (institutional parent)
                              │
                      ENSPORT VENTURES
              (the Group's dedicated sports platform)
                              │
                          ATHLIMA
        India's Festival of Sport, Business & Performance
                              │
     ┌─────────┬─────────┬────┴────┬─────────┬─────────┐
  ATHLIMAX  SYMPOSIUM  ACTIV8  AFTERHOURS  CONNECT  ATHLIMA 20
 marketplace   ideas   experience  culture  relationships  next gen
```

### Naming rules
- **ATHLIMA** — always all caps. Never *Athlima*, never *AthLima*, never *ATHLIMA 2026* except where the
  edition is specifically meant.
- **ATHLIMAX** — one word, all caps. The final X is the lime accent character.
- **THE SYMPOSIUM** — always with the definite article. The final M carries the lime accent.
- **ACTIV8** — the 8 is the lime accent character. Never *Activate*, never *Activ8*.
- **AFTERHOURS** — one word. "AFTER" white, "HOURS" in the gradient.
- **ATHLIMA CONNECT** — two words, both caps.
- **ATHLIMA 20** — the numeral, always. Long form when first introduced: *ATHLIMA 20 — 20 athletes.
  20 sports. One future.*
- **ENSPORT Ventures** and **The ENARR Group** — mixed case as shown; these are corporate marks, not
  ATHLIMA marks, and they follow their own identities.

### The sub-brand rule
Each IP has its own name, its own lock-up and, in the case of AFTERHOURS, its own colour. **None of them
have their own design system.** They are rooms in one building: same type, same grid, same spacing, same
motion. Only the accent and the imagery change. A visitor must never feel they have left ATHLIMA and
arrived somewhere else.

## 3. THE FIVE PILLARS

These are the strategic architecture, not a themed list. They are the taxonomy through which every
partner, every guest, every session and every piece of content is classified.

| Pillar | What it covers | Who it speaks to |
|---|---|---|
| **BUILD** | Infrastructure, architecture, development, sporting facilities, active cities | Developers, architects, PMCs, urban planners, infrastructure companies |
| **EQUIP** | Technology, equipment, apparel, nutrition, hydration, recovery, sports science products | Brands, manufacturers, technology companies |
| **ENABLE** | Academies, clubs, schools, universities, foundations, operators | Operators, educators, foundations |
| **PERFORM** | Athletes, coaches, high performance, sports science, medicine, fitness | Athletes, coaches, practitioners |
| **GOVERN** | Government, federations, sports authorities, policy, institutional leadership | Government, federations, policymakers |

**Website implementation:** the pillars are a real taxonomy in the CMS, not decoration. Every Journal
article, every Symposium theme, every partner and every person carries one or more pillar tags. This makes
the pillars *navigable* — a developer can filter the whole site to BUILD — which is what turns a graphic
device into an information architecture.

## 4. THE SIX IPs — POSITIONING EACH

Each IP needs a one-line proposition, a role in the ecosystem, and an emotional register. The register is
what stops six pages feeling identical.

### ATHLIMAX — *The Marketplace*
**Line:** Conversations. Partnerships. Real-world impact.
**Role:** the commercial layer. Where the sporting economy shows what it is building.
**Register:** confident, dense, business-serious. The most information-rich of the six.
**Note:** "curated, invitation-led marketplace" — never "invite-only" in public copy, never "exhibition
floor", never "stall", never "booth".

### THE SYMPOSIUM — *The Ideas*
**Line:** A higher conversation.
**Role:** the intellectual layer, and ATHLIMA's route to authority beyond December.
**Register:** the most editorial and restrained of the six. Closest to a serious journal.
**Six themes (locked):** The Next Generation · Infrastructure & Cities · Technology & Innovation ·
Investment & Economics · Policy & Governance · Culture & Impact.
**Six formats (locked):** Keynotes · Panel Discussions · Roundtables · Fireside Chats · Case Studies ·
Special Sessions.

### ACTIV8 — *The Experience*
**Line:** Play beyond the game.
**Role:** the physical layer. Where sport stops being discussed and starts being done.
**Register:** the most kinetic. The only place where motion may be fast and the imagery may be daylit.
**Six zones (locked):** Performance · Play · Recovery · Technology · Community · Live Experiences.

### AFTERHOURS — *The Culture*
**Line:** Where sport meets culture.
**Role:** the cultural and hospitality layer. The night register of the platform.
**Register:** the only place the palette changes. Violet-to-blue gradient, not lime. Warmer, looser.
**Six elements (locked):** Dining · Cocktails · Music · Culture · Networking · Entertainment.
**Signature:** The Runway — an athleisure and performance fashion show. `[TO VERIFY]` The 60ft dimension
in the source material needs confirming against the production plan.

### ATHLIMA CONNECT — *The Relationships*
**Line:** Connect before you arrive. Collaborate when you get there.
**Role:** the relationship engine, and the single strongest commercial differentiator.
**Register:** precise, mechanical, reassuring. This is the one place a diagram outperforms a photograph.
**Who it is for:** Founding Partners. It is a partner benefit, not a guest promise (decision D20).
**Four steps (locked):** Identify → Match → Pre-block → Arrive Ready.
**Mandatory disclaimer wherever Connect is described:** *Meetings are subject to guest confirmation,
availability and mutual suitability. ATHLIMA does not guarantee meetings with any specific individual or
organisation.* This is already in the source material and must survive to the website verbatim.

### ATHLIMA 20 — *The Next Generation*
**Line:** 20 athletes. 20 sports. One future. / Tomorrow plays here.
**Role:** the athlete and legacy layer, and the IP most likely to outlive the event.
**Register:** the most human and the most emotional of the six. The only place portraiture leads.
**Philosophy (locked):** *Do not wait until they become champions to tell their story. Tell it while they
are becoming one.*
**Eligibility:** `[TO VERIFY]` under 20 on 14 December 2026 — stated on the page, not buried in a form
(decision D4). Most nominees are minors; the nomination form is v2, gated on DPDP legal review.
**Twenty disciplines (locked):** Athletics · Swimming · Football · Cricket · Hockey · Tennis · Badminton ·
Table Tennis · Boxing · Wrestling · Weightlifting · Shooting · Archery · Gymnastics · Cycling · Rowing ·
Martial Arts · Equestrian · Surfing · Para Sport.

## 5. THE BRAND PERSONALITY

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Institutional | Corporate |
| Confident | Loud |
| Curated | Exclusionary |
| Ambitious | Grandiose |
| Contemporary Indian | Generically global |
| Precise | Cold |
| Cinematic | Theatrical |
| Serious | Humourless |

The line that matters most: **institutional but not corporate.** ATHLIMA should feel like an institution
being founded — with the seriousness that implies — while still feeling like it was made by people who
care about how things look.

## 6. THE INDIA QUESTION

ATHLIMA is unambiguously Indian, and the brand must feel it — but not through the obvious signifiers.

**Do:** Mumbai as a real, specific, contemporary city — the Bandra–Worli Sea Link, the skyline at night,
the Coastal Road, monsoon light, the scale of the place. Indian athletes, Indian faces, Indian institutions.
Indian English. The ambition and impatience of the country's current moment.

**Do not:** saffron-white-green, tricolour gradients, Ashoka Chakra motifs, temple architecture, henna
patterns, folk-craft borders, "Incredible India" tourism visual language, or any device that treats
Indianness as ornament.

The brochure gets this right and the website must not regress. **India appears as ambition and place, never
as decoration.**

## 7. THE ENARR / ENSPORT RELATIONSHIP ON THE WEBSITE

Currently a back-cover logo. It should be a section.

**How to express it, in one screen:**

> ATHLIMA is not an event company's event.
>
> ATHLIMA is an ENSPORT Ventures initiative within the ENARR Group, bringing together sport, business,
> capital, institutions and ideas around the future of performance in India. Built on the Group's broader
> experience across finance, enterprise, industry, media and philanthropy, ATHLIMA exists to create **the
> room** where the people shaping Indian sport can connect, collaborate and build what comes next.

*Approved by the project owner, 13 September 2026 — `08_OPERATIONS/decisions-b3-provenance.md` §4a. The
earlier "multi-generational institutional history" line is withdrawn. The public word is **initiative**,
never "IP" (§5) — "IP" stays internal, for the six sub-brands as a portfolio.*

lock-up itself.

ENSPORT: gold and white). They are **not** recoloured to lime. They appear on a black ground at modest
scale, with generous clear space, in a section that is quieter and more formal than the rest of the site.
The contrast in register is the point — it reads as provenance.

## 8. THE BRAND'S RELATIONSHIP TO TIME

ATHLIMA is a date (14–15 December 2026) and a platform (year-round). The brand must hold both without the
site becoming a countdown.

**The `phase` enum has five values** — the source of truth is `01_STRATEGY/digital-strategy.md` §5:
`foundation` · `build` · `approach` · `live` · `legacy`. They collapse into three broad states:

1. **Pre-event** (now → 13 Dec 2026) — anticipation, partner acquisition, Journal, ATHLIMA 20
   nomination and reveal.
2. **Live** (14–15 Dec 2026) — the site becomes a live surface. Programme, moments, ATHLIMA 20 class reveal.
3. **Post-event** (16 Dec 2026 →) — the archive becomes an asset. Films, outcomes, the 2026 class, and the
   opening of the 2027 cycle.

**Build consequence:** every date-sensitive component takes an explicit `phase` prop typed to the
five-value enum, not to these three states. The homepage must be
switchable between the three states by a single CMS setting, without a redeploy. A site that reads
"14–15 December 2026" in the future tense on 20 December 2026 has failed.

**And:** no countdown timer. It cheapens the register, and it makes the site worthless the moment it hits
zero.
