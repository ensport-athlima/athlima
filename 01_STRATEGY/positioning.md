## 2. THE CATEGORY WE ARE CLAIMING

ATHLIMA is not competing inside an existing category. It is naming a new one.

**Category:** *the convening platform for India's sporting economy.*

There is no incumbent. The nearest reference points are international and structurally different — the
kind of invitation-led, cross-sector convenings where an industry's decision-makers meet annually and the
value is the room rather than the programme.

The website's job is therefore **category creation, not competitive differentiation.** We are not arguing
that ATHLIMA is better than something. We are asserting that a thing which should exist does not yet, and
that this is it.

That has a direct design consequence: **the site must never look like it is competing.** No comparison
tables, no "why ATHLIMA vs X", no defensive copy. Confidence is the argument.

## 4. THE THREE PROOFS

Every positioning claim needs proof. ATHLIMA has exactly three, and the website must lean on all of them.

### PROOF 1 — THE ROOM
350 people, four stakeholder groups, cross-sector by design. The proof is the *composition* of the room,
not the fame of any individual in it. The website expresses this as a structure — who is there and why
their presence matters to each other — rather than as a wall of headshots.

### PROOF 2 — THE INSTITUTION BEHIND IT
ATHLIMA is not a promoter's event. It is an IP of **ENSPORT Ventures Pvt. Ltd.**, the dedicated sports
platform within **The ENARR Group**. This is the credibility anchor and it is currently under-used in the
source material — the brochures carry the ENARR and ENSPORT marks only on the back cover.

**On the website this must be raised.** An institutional backer is exactly what separates ATHLIMA from the
dozens of sports events a federation secretary is invited to every year. It earns a proper section, not a
footer logo.

### PROOF 3 — THE ARCHITECTURE
Five pillars, six IPs, a seven-stage partner journey, a two-day flow, a designed floor. The sheer
*structural coherence* of ATHLIMA is itself the proof that this is a considered platform and not an
occasion. The website should let people see the architecture — this is where information design earns its
place: the navigable pillar diagram, the numbered sequences, the Room composition. *(An interactive
ecosystem map would be justified rather than decorative — but only once relationship data exists. It is
v2, decision D6.)*

## 5. THE POSITIONING RISKS

| Risk | How it shows up | The guard |
|---|---|---|
| **Reads as an expo** | Floor plans, booth talk, "exhibitors", category lists as the headline | Lead with people and outcomes. The floor plan is a supporting artefact, deep in the partner journey. |
| **Reads as a conference** | Speaker grids, agenda tables, session times above the fold | Lead with the room. The programme is proof of substance, not the offer. |
| **Reads as vapour** | Big claims, no specifics, all atmosphere and no facts | Every aspirational screen is followed by a concrete one. 70% atmosphere is the visual ratio, not the evidentiary one. |
| **Reads as promotional** | Exclamation, urgency, "limited spots", countdown timers | No countdown timer on this site. Scarcity is stated once, calmly, and never sold. |
| **Over-claims** | Unconfirmed names presented as confirmed | See §6. This is the most serious risk on the entire project. |
| **Reads as crypto** | Black + neon green + glow + grid = a Web3 landing page | Governed in `03_DESIGN_SYSTEM/colour.md`. Lime is a signal, never a surface. |

## 6. THE CLAIMS DISCIPLINE — BINDING

The source material contains extensive **target, proposed and invited** lists — guests, Advisory Council
members, federations, government stakeholders, partner brands. The master brief is explicit about this,
repeatedly.

### The rule, without exception:

> **Nothing appears on the public website as confirmed until it is independently confirmed in writing.**

Specifically:

- **No named individual** appears on the site — as guest, speaker, or Advisory Council member — without
  that person's written confirmation and approval of how they are described.
- **No organisation's name or logo** appears without written permission.
- The Advisory Council is described as **proposed / invited** until each member confirms. If fewer than
  five have confirmed at launch, the Council does not get a page — it gets a single line saying it is being
  formed.
- **No partner brand** appears in any "Founding Partners" position until the agreement is signed.
- Named brand examples explored in strategy work (Campus, ARC, Skechers, Elemnt, Medallin, Ileseum, EBACO,
  Acosa, Stupa, NRS, Aquatein and others) are **internal illustrations of the partnership model**. They do
  not appear on the public website in any form.
- The ATHLIMA 20 × Dream Sports Foundation relationship, the ATHLIMA Run, ALTLIMA and the Singhania Family
  Sports Foundation are **proposed** and are not announced until confirmed.
- The ATHLIMA Run additionally requires civic, traffic, police and statutory approvals. It is not published
  in any form until those are granted.

**Why this matters more than anything else on the site:** ATHLIMA's entire proposition is credibility with
serious institutions. One federation president discovering they are listed as attending an event they never
agreed to attend does more damage than a mediocre website ever could. This risk is asymmetric and permanent.

**Implementation:** any fact awaiting confirmation is tagged `[TO VERIFY]` in the content files and is
physically absent from the build. A `[TO VERIFY]` tag must never reach production. This is checked in
`07_QA/content-qa.md` and in the launch checklist.

## 7. THE STATISTICS — HANDLE WITH CARE

The brochure carries four figures on page 02:

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| $130B — India's sports economy by 2030 (est.) | `[TO VERIFY — B4]` — needs a named, citable source and a date |
| 3X — growth in sports infrastructure investment | `[TO VERIFY — B4]` — over what period? against what baseline? |
| 600M+ — young Indians under 25 | `[TO VERIFY — B4]` — plausible; needs a census or UN source |
| Global Top 3 — sports market potential | `[TO VERIFY — B4]` — by what measure? whose projection? |

**Default if unanswered when screen 02 is built (decisions file, Part 2):** cut all four and let the screen
rest on the stakeholder grid.

These are strong and useful. They are also exactly the kind of number a sceptical investor will check.

**Rule:** every statistic on the website carries a visible source and year. A figure without a source does
not ship. If a source cannot be found, the figure is cut — the argument is strong enough without it, and
one unsourced number undermines every sourced one beside it.

## 8. TWO CONTRADICTIONS IN THE SOURCE MATERIAL — RESOLVE BEFORE BUILD

Found during reconciliation of the two brochures. Both must be settled by the project owner before the
relevant pages are built.

### 8.1 — Twenty partners or twenty-two spaces?
- *Revised brochure, p09:* "20 founding partners across six strategic pavilions" (4+3+4+3+3+3 = 20)
- *Founding Partner Prospectus, p06:* "22 premium brand spaces. 12 categories."
- *Floor plan:* 22 numbered spaces.

These are two different commercial architectures. **`[TO VERIFY — B1 — DECISION REQUIRED]`** The website can
only state one. Recommendation: the six-pavilion / 20-partner model, because it is the newer document and
because six pavilions echo the six IPs, which strengthens the whole system. But this is a commercial
decision, not a design one.

### 8.2 — 350 people or 500+ leaders?
- *Revised brochure, p05, and all master-brief material:* **350** — and the scarcity is the entire proposition.
- *Founding Partner Prospectus, p10:* "500+ CURATED LEADERS" alongside "30+ sessions".

**`[TO VERIFY — B1 — DECISION REQUIRED]`** These cannot both be true, and the difference is strategically
material: 350 is a proposition, 500+ is a number. Recommendation: **350 everywhere.** It is the more
defensible claim, it is what the positioning is built on, and "the value is not how many people attend"
sits badly next to a headline attendance figure. The 500+ figure should be removed from all material.
