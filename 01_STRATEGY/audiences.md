## 2. THE SIX DOORWAYS

The website must answer *"why should I care?"* differently for each. These are the six audience entry
points, and each gets its own opening line, its own proof, its own destination.

**"Our line" below is locked as each `/for/*` page's opening argument.** The emotional CTA *string* for
each page is locked separately in `04_CONTENT/ctas.md` §1; where the two differ (athletes, infrastructure,
brands), each wins in its own domain (decision D17).

### DOORWAY 01 — THE FOUNDER / BUSINESS LEADER
**Their question:** Where is the sporting economy actually being decided?
**Our line:** **Find the people shaping sport's next economy.**
**What they need to see:** who is in the room, cross-sector; the Founding Partner model; ATHLIMA Connect
*as a Founding Partner benefit — it is not offered to guests (decision D20)*.
**Their fear:** another networking event that wastes two days.
**The answer to that fear:** 350, not 5,000. A room composed so the people you need are in it. And if
your organisation partners, conversations arranged before you arrive.
**Destination:** Partner with ATHLIMA.

### DOORWAY 02 — THE ATHLETE / COACH / PERFORMANCE PROFESSIONAL
**Their question:** What is here for me that isn't just business people talking about us?
**Our line:** **The people building your future are in one room. So are you.**
**What they need to see:** ATHLIMA 20; ACTIV8; the performance and sports-science strand of the Symposium;
that athletes are participants, not exhibits.
**Their fear:** being decoration at a corporate event.
**The answer to that fear:** ATHLIMA 20 is a platform for athletes, run with independent selection. The
Symposium has athletes on stage, not just about them.
**Destination:** ATHLIMA 20 (the pre-window alert in v1; nomination in v2 — decision D4). No guest application (A1).

### DOORWAY 03 — THE INVESTOR / FAMILY OFFICE
**Their question:** Is there real deal flow here, or is this a party?
**Our line:** **The opportunities behind India's sporting growth, in one room.**
**What they need to see:** the Investment, Policy & Impact pavilion; the Investment & Economics theme;
the startup showcase; the composition of the founder and infrastructure cohort.
**Their fear:** a low-quality pipeline and a wasted evening.
**The answer to that fear:** curated composition, and the specific pavilion architecture.
**Destination:** Build with ATHLIMA (a partner conversation) — or understanding, and a forward. No guest application (A1).

### DOORWAY 04 — THE DEVELOPER / ARCHITECT / INFRASTRUCTURE LEADER
**Their question:** Are the people who commission and operate sporting facilities actually going to be there?
**Our line:** **Sport needs places. Meet the people who decide where they get built.**
**What they need to see:** the BUILD pillar; Infrastructure & Active Cities pavilion; federations,
government and operators in the room together.
**Their fear:** meeting other developers instead of clients.
**The answer to that fear:** the cross-sector composition is the entire design of the room.
**Destination:** Build with ATHLIMA (a partner conversation). No guest application (A1).

### DOORWAY 05 — THE GOVERNMENT / FEDERATION / INSTITUTION
**Their question:** Is this serious, and is it appropriate for us to be associated with it?
**Our line:** **Build the infrastructure around India's sporting future.**
**What they need to see:** ENSPORT and The ENARR Group; the Advisory Council *(only if confirmed — see
`04_CONTENT/people.md`; the doorway page does not depend on it)*; the Policy & Governance
theme; institutional register throughout; no commercial hard sell anywhere on their path.
**Their fear:** a commercial event using institutional names for legitimacy.
**The answer to that fear:** register, restraint, and the complete absence of pricing and ticketing.
**Destination:** Institutional enquiry — a separate, quieter contact route.
**Note:** this is the audience most sensitive to tone. Their journey must never pass through a sales page.

### DOORWAY 06 — THE BRAND
**Their question:** What can I own here that I cannot own anywhere else?
**Our line:** **Don't sponsor sport. Own a territory inside it.**
**What they need to see:** the six-level partner model; the value architecture; the partner journey; the
IP examples in principle — never with real brand names attached.
**Their fear:** paying for a logo on a wall.
**The answer to that fear:** *The value isn't measured in logos.* — the brochure's own line.
**Destination:** Partner with ATHLIMA.

## 4. HOW THE DOORWAYS APPEAR ON THE SITE

Three mechanisms, in order of importance:

### 4.1 The Doorways block on the homepage
A dedicated screen — homepage screen 06 — where the six doorways are presented as a set. The visitor
self-selects. This is the single highest-value interaction on the site because it converts a generic
visitor into a qualified one in one click.

**Design constraint:** six doors must not be a six-up card grid. They are entrances. Full-height,
type-led, revealing on hover/tap, each with its line and a real destination. See `04_CONTENT/homepage.md`.

### 4.2 Audience-shaped landing pages
Each doorway leads to a page written for that reader: their language, their proof, their CTA. These are
real routes with real URLs — shareable, indexable, and usable as the destination of a targeted outreach
email. This is where the sales team's links point.

### 4.3 Ordering within shared pages
On pages every audience sees (The World, the six IPs), the content order is fixed but the *emphasis* is
not. Where an IP serves one audience most, that audience's proof comes first.

**What we do not do in v1:** dynamic personalisation, cookie-based audience memory, or "choose your
profile" gates. Too fragile, too creepy, and it breaks sharing and SEO. The doorways are links.

## 5. THE JOURNEY MAP

```
DISCOVER            UNDERSTAND           SELF-IDENTIFY        QUALIFY            COMMIT
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Homepage       →    The World       →    Doorways        →   Audience page  →   Build with ATHLIMA
Journal        →    Six IPs         →    "this is me"    →   The proof      →   Enquire
Social / press →    The People                               ATHLIMA Connect    Nominate
LinkedIn       →    ENSPORT/ENARR                            The programme
```

**Each stage has one job:**

- **Discover** — establish scale and seriousness in under 10 seconds.
- **Understand** — make the ecosystem legible. Six IPs as one system.
- **Self-identify** — the doorways. *"That one is me."*
- **Qualify** — give that reader the specific proof they need, and pre-empt their specific fear.
- **Commit** — one emotional CTA, one functional CTA, and a form that respects their time.

**The drop-off to watch:** Understand → Self-identify. If visitors read The World and leave, the doorways
are too far down the homepage or too weak. Instrument this specifically.

## 6. WHAT EVERY AUDIENCE SHARES

Whatever door they come through, all six must leave with the same three things. If a page delivers none of
them, it should not exist.

1. **This is serious.** Institutionally backed, properly built, not a promoter's event.
2. **The room is small and deliberately composed.** I would be in it with people I could not otherwise reach.
3. **Getting in is not automatic.** It is by invitation, and there is no form (decision A1). That is the point.
