# PEOPLE
### `/the-room` · `/the-room/advisory-council` · `/about` · and the claims discipline in practice

> **Read `01_STRATEGY/positioning.md` §6 before writing a single word of this page.**
> This is the highest-risk content on the entire site. One federation president discovering they are
> listed as attending an event they never agreed to attend does more damage than a mediocre website ever
> could.

---

## THE GOVERNING RULE

> **No individual's name appears anywhere on this website until that person has confirmed, in writing,
> both their participation and how they are described.**

This page is designed to be **complete, compelling and persuasive with zero names on it.** Names, when
they arrive, strengthen it. They are not what makes it work.

That is a design constraint, and it is a gift: it forces the page to argue from *composition* rather than
from celebrity — which is, in any case, the actual proposition. *The value is not how many people attend.
The value is who you meet.*

## Section 02 — THE FOUR GROUPS

The full expansion of the homepage's compact version.

### SPORT — *Talent. Performance. Ecosystem.*
Athletes · Coaches · Federations · Academies · Sports scientists · Leagues & franchises
**What they bring to the room:** the reality of performance, and the demand that everything else exists to serve.
**What they take from it:** access to the capital, infrastructure and technology being built around them.
**Verbs:** `MEET · COLLABORATE · INVEST`

### INSTITUTIONS — *Policy. Infrastructure. Enablers.*
Government · Authorities · Universities · Sporting bodies · Public sector · Policy makers
**What they bring:** the frameworks within which everything else operates.
**What they take:** private-sector solutions to public-sector problems, in one place.
**Verbs:** `SHARE · LEARN · SHAPE POLICY`

### BUSINESS — *Brands. Innovation. Execution.*
CEOs & founders · Brands · Technology · Infrastructure · Media & entertainment · Professional services
**What they bring:** products, platforms and the capacity to execute.
**What they take:** institutional buyers, and a route to the people who make decisions.
**Verbs:** `BUILD · PARTNER · CREATE OPPORTUNITIES`

### CAPITAL — *Investment. Growth. Long-term impact.*
Investors · Family offices · Funds · Advisors · Sporting entrepreneurs · Impact capital
**What they bring:** the money that turns ambition into infrastructure.
**What they take:** visibility into a sector that is difficult to survey from outside.
**Verbs:** `BACK · SCALE · DRIVE IMPACT`

## Section 03 — THE COMPOSITION

**Signature Interaction 04.** See `02_INFORMATION_ARCHITECTURE/interaction-map.md`.

The core idea: the visitor can see **who meets whom**, and what that adjacency produces. Filterable by the
four groups and the five pillars.

**Designed to work entirely on categories, proportions and counts.**

Example content, no names required:

> **When a developer meets a federation secretary**
> A stadium gets a tenant. A federation gets a home.
>
> **When a technology founder meets a state sports authority**
> A pilot becomes a programme.
>
> **When an investor meets an academy operator**
> A regional academy becomes a network.
>
> **When a brand meets an athlete's coach**
> A sponsorship becomes a partnership.

This is the page's most persuasive content, and it requires no confirmations whatsoever.

**Sector composition** — presented as proportions of the 350, not as a list, and **labelled on the page as
the target composition** until the room is confirmed. A target presented as a room is a claim the site
cannot back.
`[TO VERIFY]` The actual target composition percentages need to be supplied by the commercial team.
Do not invent them. Until they exist, the view shows the four groups and the adjacency examples without
proportions.

## Section 04 — THE ADVISORY COUNCIL *(conditional)*

**The condition:** five or more members confirmed in writing.

### If the condition is NOT met
`/the-room/advisory-council` returns 404. `/the-room` carries exactly this, and nothing more:

> **AN ADVISORY COUNCIL IS BEING FORMED.**
>
> ATHLIMA is assembling a cross-disciplinary Advisory Council spanning high performance, sports law and
> governance, philanthropy, infrastructure, investment, sporting institutions and business — to provide
> institutional depth as the platform develops.
>
> Members will be announced as they are confirmed.

**No names. No "invited" list. No silhouettes. No count of how many have been approached.**

### If the condition IS met
Only confirmed members appear. Each with: name exactly as they spell it, their real current title, their
organisation, a portrait they have approved, and one line on what they bring.

**Framing copy:**
> The Council is not ceremonial. Its purpose is to make ATHLIMA smarter.

**Never on this page:** anyone described as "invited", "proposed", "in conversation" or "expected".
A proposed council member is not council content — they are a sales conversation.

# THE PEOPLE CONTENT CHECKLIST

Before any page containing a person's name goes live:

- [ ] Written confirmation of participation is on file
- [ ] The person has approved their name spelling, title and organisation
- [ ] The person has approved their portrait
- [ ] The person has approved the one-line description of them
- [ ] Their title is current, not the one they held when first approached
- [ ] Their organisation has confirmed the use of its name
- [ ] Nothing on the page describes anyone as invited, proposed, expected or in conversation
- [ ] No logo appears without written permission
- [ ] The page reads well and argues successfully with the names removed

**That last one is the real test.** If the page collapses without the names, it was never an argument —
it was a guest list.
