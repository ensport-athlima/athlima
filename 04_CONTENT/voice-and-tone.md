# VOICE AND TONE

---

## 1. THE VOICE IN ONE LINE

> **The confident restraint of an institution that does not need to convince you.**

ATHLIMA writes the way a serious organisation speaks when it already knows it is serious. Short sentences.
Concrete nouns. No pleading. No hype. The reader is treated as someone who will work it out.

---

## 2. THE FIVE VOICE RULES

### 01 — STATE. DON'T SELL.
> ✗ *ATHLIMA is an unmissable opportunity to connect with India's most influential sporting leaders.*
> ✓ *350 people. The right people.*

The second sentence is more persuasive because it is not trying to be.

### 02 — SHORT SENTENCES CARRY WEIGHT.
Vary the length, but let the important sentences be very short. A sentence on its own line is the strongest
device the voice has.

> *The ecosystem is active.*
> *But not connected.*

### 03 — CONCRETE OVER ABSTRACT.
> ✗ *A holistic ecosystem of synergistic stakeholders driving transformative outcomes.*
> ✓ *Developers do not routinely meet sporting operators. Federations do not routinely meet capital.*

Name the actual people and the actual problem. Specificity is the difference between a claim and an
argument.

### 04 — NO SUPERLATIVES. USE FACTS INSTEAD.
> ✗ *India's most prestigious sporting platform.*
> ✓ *14–15 December 2026. The St. Regis Mumbai. 350 people.*

If ATHLIMA is what it says it is, the facts do the work.

### 05 — INDIAN ENGLISH, CONSISTENTLY.
*organisation · programme · centre · realise · colour · behaviour · analyse · travelling · towards*
Not *organization, program, center, realize, color*.
Lakh and crore where the audience is Indian and the figure is Indian. Dates as `14–15 December 2026`,
never `December 14-15, 2026`.

## 3. THE BANNED WORD LIST

Every one of these appears in the average event website and none appears on this one.

### Empty adjectives
world-class · cutting-edge · state-of-the-art · premier · unparalleled · unrivalled · bespoke · seamless ·
holistic · immersive · transformative · game-changing · revolutionary ·
disruptive · next-level · best-in-class · one-of-a-kind · must-attend · unmissable · iconic · legendary

### Business filler
synergy · leverage *(as a verb)* · unlock *(as a verb — **except** in the brochure-sourced five-verb row
on Home screen 03, which is verbatim ATHLIMA copy)* · ideate · deep dive · circle back · touch base ·
value-add · thought leader *(the person; "thought leadership" as an activity is fine)* · ecosystem play ·
North Star · low-hanging fruit · move the needle · at scale · going forward · space *(as in "the sports
space")*

### Hype
Don't miss out · Limited spots · Hurry · Act now · Exclusive access *(when it is not)* · VIP *(except for
the actual VIP Lounge on the floor plan)* · Once in a lifetime · The future is here · Are you ready? ·
Get ready

### AI tells
*In today's fast-paced world* · *In an era where* · *It's not just X — it's Y* · *Whether you're X or Y* ·
*Let's dive in* · *At its core* · *The bottom line* · *Here's the thing* · *And that's not all* ·
*More than just* · *A testament to* · *Nestled in* · *Boasts* · *Elevate your* · *Embark on a journey* ·
*Delve into* · *Navigate the landscape*

### Wrong-register words for ATHLIMA specifically
attendees *(→ guests)* · delegates *(→ guests)* · tickets *(→ applications)* · booth · stall *(→ space,
pavilion)* · sponsor *(→ partner)* · expo · trade show · networking event · conference *(ATHLIMA contains
a symposium; it is not a conference)* · workshop · session *(fine for the Symposium, wrong for everything
else)*

### The word that is not banned: "curated"
**"Curated" is permitted** (decision D31). It is ATHLIMA's own word for its own selection process — *a
curated, invitation-led platform*, *350 curated decision-makers*, *A CURATED MARKETPLACE* — and it
describes something ATHLIMA actually does: it chooses who is in the room. It is banned on most event sites
because those sites do not curate; ATHLIMA does. Use it for ATHLIMA's selection of guests, partners and
content. Do not use it as a general-purpose adjective for things ATHLIMA has not selected ("a curated
skyline").

### The source-material override
Where a line is **verbatim from the ATHLIMA brochures**, it takes precedence over this list. ATHLIMA's own
words are the voice. This applies only to exact quotations, never to newly written copy in the same
register, and every such line is marked *brochure-sourced* in the content files. `[TO VERIFY]` The
brochure PDFs are image-only; every *brochure-sourced* mark in `04_CONTENT/` follows the repository's own
attributions and must be checked verbatim against the printed page before launch.

### The two words to be careful with
**"Elevate"** — it is one of ATHLIMA's three brand words and it appears in the lock-up. That is exactly why
it must not be used as a verb anywhere else. *Elevate your brand* undermines *Connect · Collaborate ·
Elevate*.

**"Journey"** — used in the source material for the partner journey, which is a legitimate structural term.
Never used metaphorically. Nobody embarks on a journey.

## 4. TONE BY CONTEXT

The voice is constant. The tone shifts.

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Declarative, weighty | *The business of sport. The future of India.* |
| The diagnosis | Direct, slightly blunt | *India is ready. Sport isn't connected.* |
| The IP pages | Assured, specific | *A curated, invitation-led marketplace.* |
| ATHLIMA 20 | Warm, human — the only place the voice softens | *Do not wait until they become champions to tell their story.* |
| AFTERHOURS | Looser, more atmospheric | *The day inspires. The night celebrates.* |
| Partner pages | Business-serious, respectful | *The value isn't measured in logos.* |
| Institutional pages | Formal, restrained, unhurried | *An ENSPORT Ventures initiative · ENARR Group* |
| Forms and microcopy | Plain, helpful, human | *We read every application. You'll hear from us by 30 September.* |
| Errors | Direct, never cute | *That email address doesn't look right. Check it and try again.* |
| The Journal | Editorial, longer sentences permitted | Full paragraphs. Real argument. Sources. |

**The one place the voice must not be austere:** forms and error messages. A cold error message is not
institutional, it is rude.

## 6. THE PEOPLE RULES

- **Never** name an individual who has not confirmed in writing. See `01_STRATEGY/positioning.md` §6.
- Names are given in full, with their real title and organisation, spelled exactly as they spell them.
  Check twice.
- No honorifics unless the person uses them.
- Athletes are named with their discipline, never with a nickname the site invented.
- Advisory Council members are described as *proposed* or *invited* until confirmed. If that reads
  awkwardly, the answer is not to publish yet.

---

## 7. THE NUMBERS RULES

- Every statistic carries a **visible source and year**. A figure without a source does not ship.
- Numerals for anything countable: *350 people*, *20 athletes*, *6 pavilions*.
- Words for anything under ten in running prose: *five pillars*, *two days*.
- Indian numbering where the audience and figure are Indian: *₹1.5 crore*, not *₹15 million*.
- En dashes for ranges: *14–15 December*, *2026–2027*.
- Never round up to sound better. *350* is not *"over 300"*, and it is certainly not *"hundreds of"*.

---

## 8. THE READ-ALOUD TEST

Every sentence on the site gets read out loud before it ships.

**Reject it if:**
- It would be embarrassing to say to a federation president's face
- It sounds like a press release
- It contains a word from §3
- You run out of breath
- You could delete a third of it without losing anything
- It could appear on any other website with the name swapped

**The final check on any page:** delete the three weakest sentences. If the page is now better, delete
three more.
