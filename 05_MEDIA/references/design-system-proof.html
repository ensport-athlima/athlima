07MOTION

Five curves, six durations, three staggers. Hover a card to run its curve at --dur-slow. No bounce, no elastic, no overshoot. Only transform and opacity animate on scroll. Under prefers-reduced-motion every transition on this sheet is instant — that is the safety net; gsap.matchMedia() is the strategy.

09THE RULES THIS SHEET OBEYS
Count the lime

In any viewport of this sheet, the section marker and the APPLY button are chrome. Everything else lime is content and must not exceed three. The colour section is the deliberate exception — it is a specimen of lime, not a page.

What is not here

No glow. No gradient text. No glassmorphism. No rounded card grids with shadows. No purple or blue outside the AFTERHOURS swatch and the ENARR plate. No emoji. No icon fonts. No second accent colour. No value that is not a token.
